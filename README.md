# Symbolic Collapse Model

## Overview
This repository provides a mathematical framework for analyzing symbolic collapse pressure within complex communication systems, societies, and individual cognition. The model is designed to help understand, simulate, and potentially mitigate the effects of information overload, symbolic abstraction, polarization, and breakdowns in shared understanding.

## The Symbolic Collapse Pressure Equation

The core equation of the model is:

\[\mathcal{C} = \frac{I \cdot S \cdot P}{T \cdot E}\]

### Variables Explained:
- **\(\mathcal{C}\)**: **Collapse Pressure** — indicates systemic symbolic strain. Higher values signify greater instability.
- **\(I\)**: **Information Load** — total quantity and speed of information inputs.
- **\(S\)**: **Symbolic Abstraction** — complexity of symbolic layers (e.g., memes, jargon, legal language, AI communication).
- **\(P\)**: **Polarization** — degree of symbolic division or tribalization.
- **\(T\)**: **Transmission Fidelity** — accuracy and clarity of symbolic exchange.
- **\(E\)**: **Epistemic Coherence** — level of agreement on fundamental truths.

## Collapse Threshold

Collapse is indicated when:

\[\mathcal{C} \geq \Psi(t, T, E)\]

Where:

\[\Psi(t, T, E) = \alpha \cdot T \cdot E \cdot f(t)\]

- **\(\Psi\)**: Adaptive collapse threshold.
- **\(\alpha\)**: Normalization constant.
- **\(f(t)\)**: Time-dependent function reflecting historical entropy or systemic resilience.

## Purpose and Application

This model allows researchers, policymakers, educators, and analysts to:
- Assess vulnerability to symbolic collapse at individual, organizational, and societal levels.
- Simulate scenarios for intervention and stabilization.
- Identify critical thresholds before symbolic breakdown occurs.
