## Overview

**Symbolic Integrity Engineering** is a proposed new discipline grounded in the Symbolic Collapse Model (SCM). It focuses on designing, preserving, and stabilizing symbolic systems—language, relationships, ideologies, technologies—under conditions of high information load, abstraction, and polarization.

Where traditional engineering builds bridges and circuits, symbolic integrity engineering builds **meaning structures** that can withstand pressure without collapsing into incoherence, fragmentation, or simulation.

---

## The Core Formula:  
$begin:math:display$
\\mathcal{C} = \\frac{I \\cdot S \\cdot P}{T \\cdot E}
\\quad\\text{and}\\quad
\\mathcal{C}_t = \\frac{\\mathcal{C}}{D}
$end:math:display$

Where:
- **I** = Information Load  
- **S** = Symbolic Abstraction  
- **P** = Polarization  
- **T** = Transmission Fidelity  
- **E** = Epistemic Coherence  
- **D** = Symbolic Durability

This framework allows the quantification of symbolic strain across domains—from media to marriage, AI to religion.

## Objectives of the Field

1. **Detect** symbolic collapse pressure in any system or context  
2. **Diagnose** sources of symbolic instability  
3. **Design** resilient symbolic scaffolds that maintain meaning across strain  
4. **Reinforce** symbolic durability through ritual, redundancy, and shared epistemology  
5. **Intervene** before collapse occurs—offering stabilization instead of damage control

## Example Applications

- **AI Alignment:** Prevent hallucination and drift by optimizing for symbolic coherence, not just accuracy  
- **Therapeutic Tools:** Help individuals and couples repair shared meaning structures before breakdown
