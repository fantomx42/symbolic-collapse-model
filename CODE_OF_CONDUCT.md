# Code of Conduct

## Mission
This project exists to help researchers, educators, developers, and thinkers model symbolic collapse and epistemic instability with clarity and responsibility. We believe understanding shared meaning and information stress is essential to sustaining healthy societies.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.  
- Challenge ideas, not people.  
- Acknowledge contributions and cite when extending or adapting ideas.  
- Assume good faith and engage in epistemically honest dialogue.  

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination.  
- Deliberate misrepresentation or malicious distortion of the model.  
- Commercial exploitation without consent or proper attribution.  
- Spam, trolling, or disruptive behavior.  

## Reporting
To report concerns or violations, open a GitHub issue with the label **`conduct`** or email the repository maintainer.

Thank you for helping protect the symbolic integrity of this work.
